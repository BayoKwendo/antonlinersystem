## Full description  (limit 4000 chars — this is ~1,350)

SmartDrill Driver is the app for drilling contractors in the SmartDrill
ecosystem. It connects registered drillers with customers who need a borehole
and gives you everything to run a job from the moment it is assigned to the
moment it is complete.

Go online, get matched to work near you, and manage every job in one place.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Go online for work — flip a single switch to go online so the platform can
  match you to jobs and customers can find you nearby.

• Get matched to jobs near you — receive borehole drilling requests that the
  platform assigns based on your location.

• See the full job at a glance — customer name, service type and site address,
  with a map of the customer's location, plus the total, deposit and amount
  paid for the order.

• Contact the customer — call the customer directly from the job with one tap.

• Share your live location — while you are online and on a job, the app shares
  your live position so the customer can follow your arrival in real time. This
  runs as a foreground service with an ongoing notification whenever you are
  online, and stops the moment you go offline.

• Move the job forward — advance each job through its stages: on my way →
  arrival (confirmed by the customer) → job started → completed.

• Stay in the loop — push notifications keep you up to date on new jobs and
  status changes.

Sign in is for registered drilling providers only. From assignment to
completion, SmartDrill Driver keeps your drilling work organised, transparent
and efficient.
